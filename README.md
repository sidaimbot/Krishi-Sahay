**Krishi Sahay** is a modern AI-powered platform designed to bridge the gap between advanced technology and traditional farming. By leveraging the speed of **Vite** and the power of **Node.js**, this application provides farmers with instant answers to their agricultural queries, helping them make data-driven decisions for better yield and sustainability.

## 🚀 Why Krishi Sahay?

Farming requires timely information. Krishi Sahay serves as a digital companion that offers:
* **Instant Expert Advice:** No need to wait for an expert visit; get answers about soil, seeds, and seasons instantly.
* **Accessibility:** Designed with a simple interface for ease of use in rural areas.
* **Speed:** Optimized with Vite for fast loading even on 3G/4G mobile networks.

## ✨ Key Features

* **🤖 AI Kisan Bot:** A conversational AI that understands natural language queries regarding crop management, fertilizers, and pest control.
* **🌿 Crop Disease Diagnosis:** (Feature/Planned) Image-based analysis to identify plant diseases from photos.
* **🌦️ Weather & Forecasting:** Real-time local weather updates to help plan sowing and harvesting.
* **📈 Market Insights:** Current market prices (Mandi rates) to help farmers sell at the right time.
* **🗣️ Voice Integration:** Voice-to-text support for farmers who prefer speaking over typing.
